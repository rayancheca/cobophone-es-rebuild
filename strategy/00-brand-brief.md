# 00 — Brand positioning brief

## Who CoboPhone is

CoboPhone is the **fastest, most-trusted electronics repair brand in Madrid** — twenty years of receipts, a workshop you can walk into in Polígono Cobo Calleja, and a B2B parts arm that quietly supplies repair shops across Spain. It is two businesses inside one storefront: a high-trust consumer repair service running on a 40-minute turnaround, and a parts wholesaler embedded in the largest Chinese-import wholesale district in Europe.

## Who it serves

- **The pressed consumer.** Cracked screen, dead battery, drowned charging port. Their phone is their workday and their family. They want a clear price, a fast turnaround, and someone they can trust with their data.
- **The Madrid mail-in customer.** Lives outside Fuenlabrada but still wants a Madrid-grade repair. Needs frictionless logistics.
- **The Spain-wide mail-in customer.** Any city, with prepaid Correos / SEUR label.
- **The B2B repair-shop owner.** Spanish or Chinese-speaking. Needs parts that ship overnight, prices that hold against bulk imports, and an account they can reorder from in five clicks.
- **The bulk-resale buyer.** Knows CoboPhone for Redmi / Realme / TCL — the affordable Android tier.

## Why CoboPhone wins

1. **The 40-minute promise.** A real operational commitment, not marketing copy. It's the single strongest asset on the brand and it should be in the headline.
2. **Transparent pricing.** Most competitors hide behind "Pide presupuesto." We expose price ranges per model × per repair, on a page that is itself the conversion engine.
3. **Same-day diagnostic.** Free. Reciprocity that compounds.
4. **Three-month warranty.** With actual terms written in plain Spanish, not buried PDFs.
5. **Multi-device expertise.** Mobile, tablet, laptop, console, watch, TV, e-scooter. The brand that fixes everything tech.
6. **Spain-wide reach.** Mail-in plus 11 Madrid-area free-pickup zones.
7. **The Cobo Calleja location.** Inside the parts ecosystem. Fastest sourcing in Spain. A real moat.
8. **Twenty years.** That's not marketing. That's a survival signal.

## What feeling the brand should leave

- Relief: "this is going to be fine."
- Competence: "they've seen this exact problem before, probably this morning."
- Speed: "they actually do mean 40 minutes."
- No surprises: "the price they quoted is the price I pay."
- Quiet professionalism — not loud, not branded-out, not desperately friendly.

## The voice

- Spanish-first, native Madrileño register.
- **Tú** form, not usted. Modern, friendly, matches the repair-shop floor.
- Short sentences. Active verbs. Specific numbers.
- "40 minutos" beats "rápido." "3 meses de garantía" beats "garantía completa." "20 años" beats "amplia experiencia."
- Forbidden words: innovador, revolucionario, soluciones, experiencia única, calidad premium, líder, transformar, vanguardia, partner.
- Occasional warmth where it earns it. "Sí, sabemos cómo llegar es un poco lío. Por eso lo explicamos bien."

## The single sentence

> Reparamos tu móvil en 40 minutos. Sin sorpresas. Con garantía. Desde 2005.

## What this is not

- It is not a startup brand.
- It is not a luxury brand.
- It is not a youthful Gen-Z brand.
- It is not a corporate-IT-services brand.

It is a **competent local workshop with national-grade infrastructure**, told with the design discipline of Apple Support and the type-driven calm of Linear.
