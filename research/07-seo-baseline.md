# CoboPhone — Spanish Local SEO Baseline (May 2026)

Research agent: R7. Scope: map the search landscape CoboPhone has to win in to justify the pitch-quality rebuild. All queries run in Spanish from a Madrid context. Citations are inline URLs.

---

## 1. High-intent / commercial queries

### 1.1 "reparar pantalla móvil Madrid"
**Top 5 organic:**
1. Mundo del Móvil — https://www.mundodelmovil.com/reparar-moviles — multi-store Madrid chain, free diagnosis, sub-1hr repairs
2. Fixel Madrid — https://fixelmovil.com/ — same-day repair, free pickup/delivery within M-30
3. Reparación Móvil — https://reparacionmovil.es/ — express repair brand, 30–60min, within M-30
4. Phone House Reparaciones (chain) — https://reparaciones.phonehouse.es/cambiar-pantalla-movil-madrid — national franchise, locator-style page
5. Reparamos Ya — https://reparamosya.es/repara-tu-movil/

Maps pack: Yes — dominated by mom-and-pop independents (MadrizPhone, iRepairPhone, Mundo del Móvil) plus Phone House. No platform aggregators (no Wallapop, no Yelp) in the pack. Wallapop and `madrid.place` aggregator appear in regular organic positions 9–10.

PAA (typical): "¿Cuánto cuesta cambiar la pantalla del móvil?", "¿Dónde reparar pantalla móvil barato en Madrid?", "¿Cuánto tarda en cambiar la pantalla?", "¿Tienen garantía las reparaciones?"

Pricing on top 3: **None** show a price. Mundo del Móvil and Fixel use "presupuesto gratuito" / quote-based UX (verified via WebFetch). Reparación Móvil's homepage is brand navigation, no prices.

### 1.2 "cambiar pantalla iPhone Madrid"
**Top 5 organic:** iRepairPhone (https://irepairphone.es/reparar-iphone), PCMadrid (https://www.pcmadrid.es/reparar-iphone-en-madrid/), iFixRapid (https://www.ifixrapid.com/cambiar-pantalla-iphone), Apple Support (https://support.apple.com/iphone/repair), Europa 3G Madrid (https://www.europa3gmadrid.com/22-reparar-iphone).

Maps pack: Yes — Apple Authorized Service Providers + mom-and-pop. Apple's own support page steals a top-5 spot.

PAA: "¿Cuánto cuesta cambiar pantalla iPhone original?", "¿Apple cambia la pantalla en el día?", "¿Es mejor cambiar pantalla en Apple o tienda externa?"

Pricing: iRepairPhone lists individual model prices (e.g. iPhone 15 screen €74.38). MadrizPhone advertises "PANTALLA 85€ en el ACTO" for iPhone 11 in the title tag itself — visible right in the SERP. Europa 3G hides pricing behind quote (verified via WebFetch).

### 1.3 "reparar pantalla Samsung Madrid"
**Top 5:** Fixel (https://fixelmovil.com/collections/reparar-samsung), iRepairPhone (https://irepairphone.es/reparar-samsung), Europa 3G (https://www.europa3gmadrid.com/157-reparar-samsung), MadrizPhone (https://www.madrizphone.com/servicio-tecnico/reparacion-moviles-samsung/), Mundo del Móvil (https://www.mundodelmovil.com/reparar-moviles/reparar-samsung).

Maps pack: Yes — same independents dominate plus Samsung's official Galaxy Repair (https://www.samsung.com/es/support/galaxy-repair/) appears in position 8 of organic.

Pricing: Quote-based for top 3. Samsung official offers door-to-door service in Madrid + Barcelona ("dentro de cinco días hábiles") which is a meaningful trust anchor competing for the same query.

### 1.4 "reparar pantalla Xiaomi Madrid"
**Top 5:** Reparación Móvil (https://reparacionmovil.es/reparar-xiaomi), Europa 3G (https://www.europa3gmadrid.com/94-reparar-xiaomi), iRepairPhone (https://irepairphone.es/reparar-xiaomi), RIM Mobile, Mundo del Móvil.

**CoboPhone appears at position ~10** with https://cobophone.es/reparar-xiaomi — the only top-10 appearance in a city-level branded query.

Pricing: Xiaomi's own page exists (https://www.mi.com/es/support/spare-parts-price/) — Xiaomi publishes a parts-price calculator, so for Xiaomi specifically the search is more price-aware than the iPhone/Samsung searches.

### 1.5 "cambiar batería móvil Madrid"
**Top 5:** Europa 3G blog (https://www.europa3gmadrid.com/blog/cambiar-bateria-movil-precio/), Phone House (https://reparaciones.phonehouse.es/averia/bateria), QuickFix La Vaguada (https://www.quickfixcenter.com/), Móvil Man Europa Sol, Mundo del Móvil.

Maps pack: Yes — heavy chain presence (Phone House) plus Norauto (https://www.norauto.es/e/easy-battery-bateria-domicilio-madrid.html) — Norauto, a car-services chain, is now indexing for mobile battery at-home service.

Related searches: "cambiar batería móvil precio", "cambiar batería iPhone Madrid", "batería móvil a domicilio Madrid".

### 1.6 "reparación móviles Fuenlabrada" — **CoboPhone's home turf**

CoboPhone has **three distinct URLs in the top 10**: `/reparar-movil-en-fuenlabrada`, `/reparacion-de-moviles-fuenlabrada`, `/reparacion-moviles-fuenlabrada`. This is a doorway-page pattern — Google has not yet penalized but it is a duplicate-content risk worth flagging for the rebuild.

Maps pack: Yes, mom-and-pop dominates. iRiparo is the only mini-chain (national franchise).

## 4. Competitive landscape

### 4.1 Five most repeated competitors

1. **Europa 3G Madrid** — https://www.europa3gmadrid.com/ — appears in 9 of 13 retail queries. Madrid-wide chain, strongest brand presence overall.
2. **Mundo del Móvil** — https://www.mundodelmovil.com/ — appears in 8 of 13. Multi-store, strongest "general phone repair" rank.
3. **iRepairPhone** — https://irepairphone.es/ — appears in 7 of 13. Avenida América specialist, strong on iPhone/Samsung/Xiaomi.
4. **Phone House Reparaciones** — https://reparaciones.phonehouse.es/ — appears in 6 of 13. National chain with city-locator-page SEO.
5. **Reparación Móvil** — https://reparacionmovil.es/ — appears in 5 of 13. Madrid express-repair brand.

Honorable mentions: Fixel (3 query top-5s), MadrizPhone (3, including a Maps-pack favorite), Doctor Voltio (2 strong on consoles), Phone House on city pages.

Verdict: CoboPhone owns its hyperlocal South-Madrid identity and its Cobo Calleja B2B identity. It has not penetrated the Madrid-wide head terms, where Europa 3G + Mundo del Móvil + iRepairPhone dominate.

### 4.3 Gap between top-ranking sites and CoboPhone

Verified via WebFetch on cobophone.es, mundodelmovil.com, fixelmovil.com, europa3gmadrid.com, reparacionmovil.es:

- **Pricing**: All five sites (including CoboPhone) are quote-based. None of the top 3 in any commercial query show a printed price list. iRepairPhone and MadrizPhone are the exceptions — they show per-model prices and that visibility appears to help SERP CTR (MadrizPhone gets the SERP title to literally read "PANTALLA 85€").
- **Speed claims**: Top sites lead with "45 min", "1 hora", "30 min", "same day". CoboPhone says "40 minutos" — competitive but not visually punchy on the homepage.
- **Warranty**: Top sites all advertise 6–12 months. Samsung official offers parity. CoboPhone's homepage does not surface this prominently.
- **Pickup/delivery within M-30**: Fixel, MadrizPhone, Mundo del Móvil all advertise free door-to-door within M-30. CoboPhone is *outside* M-30 (Fuenlabrada) so it cannot honestly compete on this — but it can flip the angle: B2B wholesale shipping nationwide.
- **Service breadth**: CoboPhone already lists phone, tablet, smartwatch, laptop, console, scooter, TV — broader than most competitors. This is undersold on the homepage.
- **B2B / wholesale**: CoboPhone owns `tienda.cobophone.es` — a near-unique asset. None of the top retail competitors have a wholesale e-commerce property. This is the biggest under-leveraged moat.

### 4.4 Ten keyword clusters to target

Ranked by `conversion intent × competition gap` (where CoboPhone has a realistic shot):

1. **Cobo Calleja wholesale B2B** — `repuestos móviles cobo calleja`, `mayorista pantallas Madrid`, `mayorista repuestos iPhone Madrid`. CoboPhone already #1 on the head term. Build out long-tail with brand × part type.
2. **South-Madrid suburb pages** — `reparar móvil [Getafe|Leganés|Alcorcón|Móstoles|Parla|Humanes|Villaverde|Usera|Pinto]`. Pattern already works; consolidate the duplicate Fuenlabrada URLs and extend.
3. **Xiaomi Madrid** — `reparar pantalla Xiaomi Madrid`, `cambiar batería Xiaomi`, model-level pages. CoboPhone is already in top 10; Xiaomi is the brand where Spanish budget-conscious users search most.
4. **Console repair South Madrid** — `reparar PS5 Fuenlabrada`, `reparar Xbox Getafe`, `reparar Nintendo Switch Leganés`. The console specialists (Hospital de Consolas, Doctor Voltio) are Madrid-centro brands; the south is unclaimed.
5. **Patinete eléctrico South Madrid** — same pattern. Scooter specialists cluster around Retiro/centro; suburb repair is an open seam.
6. **Tablet/iPad South Madrid** — long-tail by model.
7. **Batería iPhone / Samsung Madrid** — head terms where CoboPhone is absent. Competing here requires pricing transparency to beat Phone House and Norauto.
8. **iPhone model-level pages** — every top competitor has them; CoboPhone needs an iPhone 15/16/17 family of pages with prices to compete.
9. **Mojado / agua / drop damage** — `reparar móvil mojado Madrid`, `móvil cayó agua reparar`. High-intent emergency searches, less crowded.
10. **B2B services for repair shops** — `pantallas iPhone al por mayor`, `OEM pantallas Samsung mayorista`, `tarifas distribuidor reparación móvil`. The national B2B distributors (Preciosadictos, iLevante) are the wall to climb — CoboPhone's physical Cobo Calleja location is the differentiator.

## 5. Hreflang opportunity analysis

### 5.1 English (en)

- Madrid has ~3.4M residents, ~1M international (29%) per idealista 2025 data (https://www.idealista.com/en/news/lifestyle-in-spain/2025/03/31/836026-madrid-s-population-in-2025-how-many-people-live-in-spain-s-capital).
- English-fluent concentration is in **Salamanca, Chamberí, Retiro, Centro/Chueca, Malasaña** — but only ~15% of residents in Centro/Salamanca are fluent in English per the same source set.
- These zones are **inside M-30**. CoboPhone is in Fuenlabrada — 20 km south. The expat searching for phone repair will almost certainly use a centro shop (Mundo del Móvil Las Ventas, iRepairPhone Cartagena, Fixel San Bernardo).
- Tourist searches ("repair iPhone Madrid", "phone repair near me Madrid") are intercepted by Apple Support, Phone House, and the centro independents. CoboPhone cannot win those geographically.
- B2B English: international wholesale buyers visiting Cobo Calleja absolutely search in English ("wholesale phone parts Spain", "mobile parts wholesaler Madrid"). The Chinese-traders-Spain-gateway-to-Europe article (https://sg.news.yahoo.com/chinese-traders-spain-gateway-europe-161408964.html) confirms Cobo Calleja is a known import hub for non-Spanish buyers.

**Recommendation on `en`**: Yes — but only for the **`tienda.cobophone.es` B2B subdomain**, not the retail-repair site. The repair site is geographically wrong for English-speaking expats; the wholesale site is geographically perfect for international B2B traffic from outside Spain.

### 5.2 Chinese Simplified (zh-Hans)

- Cobo Calleja hosts Spain's largest Chinese community with ~10,000 Chinese-origin workers and 377 of 800 businesses Chinese-run (https://www.telemadrid.es/programas/telenoticias-fin-de-semana/Cobo-Calleja-corazon-comercio-chino-2-2156504331--20190907031113.html).
- These are the literal next-door customers for CoboPhone's B2B wholesale arm. They are also a meaningful B2C base for retail repair.
- Chinese B2B buyers in this corridor regularly conduct sourcing research in Chinese — supermarket signage in the polygon is already bilingual zh/es (per TikTok evidence in research).
- Spanish-language Google still serves them for retail intent, but for B2B sourcing, zh-Hans pages capture a real audience that competitors are not targeting. None of the top-5 repeated competitors (Europa 3G, Mundo del Móvil, iRepairPhone, Phone House, Reparación Móvil) have a Chinese version.

**Recommendation on `zh-Hans`**: Yes — and this is the **most defensible hreflang investment** CoboPhone can make. The intersection of (a) literal physical proximity to Spain's largest Chinese B2B community, (b) zero competitor coverage in Chinese, and (c) high-ticket wholesale buyers makes zh-Hans a unique moat. Scope it to `tienda.cobophone.es` first; consider zh-Hans coverage on retail brand/about pages second.

### 5.3 Final hreflang call

Build `es-ES` (default) + `en` + `zh-Hans` for `tienda.cobophone.es` wholesale. Build only `es-ES` for the retail repair pages — adding English there is a low-ROI translation tax given competitor geography.
